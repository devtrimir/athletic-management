## Plan (Gate 1)

### Scope
Enable seamless lifecycle transitions between players (Members) and Coaches under a single police PNO identity. Allow personnel to serve as Player-Coaches in tournaments, resolve PNO uniqueness validation when linking Coach to Member, provide 1-click workflows to register Members as Coaches and generate athlete profiles for unlinked coaches, and unify the Coach Playing Career Achievements section to display both verified departmental tournament medals and pre-recruitment records.

### Risk + mitigation
- **Risk:** Someone enters a random Member ID to steal their PNO when creating a coach.
  **Mitigation:** `StoreCoachRequest` explicitly checks that if `member_id` is passed, the submitted PNO must match `Member::find($memberId)->pno`. Mismatch returns 422.
- **Risk:** Tournament medals get mixed with coached squad medals.
  **Mitigation:** Coached squad medals are strictly calculated from `CoachAssignment`, while personal playing career medals are queried from `participations.member_id`. They remain in separate visual and logical sections.

## Gate 3 — Summary

### What changed
- **Identity & Validation:** Personnel lifecycle transitions between Member (Athlete) and Coach now share the same police PNO identity safely. Validation rules prevent arbitrary PNO hijacking while permitting shared identity when linked.
- **Workflow & Linking:** Unlinked coaches can generate an athlete profile in 1 click (`POST coaches/{coach}/generate-athlete-profile`). Athletes can transition to coaches via `coaches.create?member_id={id}` with all personal and service records pre-filled and PNO locked.
- **Tournament & Dual Roles:** When personnel serve simultaneously as coach and competitor in the same team session, both member and coach rosters identify them with a `Player-Coach` badge.
- **Achievements Separation:** In Coach Profile under Achievements:
  1. *Coached Player Achievements*: Displays team/squad achievements won under this coach's training.
  2. *Playing Career Achievements*: Displays personal medals won as an athlete (from linked member tournament participations) along with prior/pre-recruitment achievements. Coached medals and personal medals never mix.

### Key Decisions & Rationale
- **Single Source of Truth for PNO:** Rather than relaxing PNO uniqueness everywhere, `UniquePnoAcrossPeople` explicitly ignores only the linked identity (`$ignoreMemberId`), and `StoreCoachRequest` enforces that `pno` matches the linked member. This guarantees cross-table uniqueness across all distinct personnel.
- **Hybrid Playing Achievements:** Coaches who joined police through sports quotas retain pre-recruitment national/state medals (`CoachPlayingAchievement`), but once recruited and competing in departmental tournaments, those medals flow from official `Achievement` records. The UI presents both under clearly separated sub-headers.
