### Risk + mitigation
- **Risk:** Someone enters a random Member ID to steal their PNO when creating a coach.
  **Mitigation:** `StoreCoachRequest` explicitly checks that if `member_id` is passed, the submitted PNO must match `Member::find($memberId)->pno`. Mismatch returns 422.
- **Risk:** Tournament medals get mixed with coached squad medals.
  **Mitigation:** Coached squad medals are strictly calculated from `CoachAssignment`, while personal playing career medals are queried from `participations.member_id`. They remain in separate visual and logical sections.

## Gate 3 — Summary

### What changed
- **Identity & Validation:** Personnel lifecycle transitions between Member (Athlete) and Coach now share the same police PNO identity safely. Validation rules prevent arbitrary PNO hijacking while permitting shared identity when linked.
- **Workflow & Linking:** Unlinked coaches can generate an athlete profile in 1 click (`POST coaches/{coach}/generate-athlete-profile`). Athletes can transition to coaches via `coaches.create?member_id={id}` with all personal and service records pre-filled and PNO locked.
- **Tournament & Dual Roles:** When personnel serve simultaneously as coach and competitor in the same team session, both member and coach rosters identify them with a `Player-Coach` badge.
- **Achievements Separation:** In Coach Profile under Achievements:
  1. *Coached Player Achievements*: Displays team/squad achievements won under this coach's training.
  2. *Playing Career Achievements*: Displays personal medals won as an athlete (from linked member tournament participations) along with prior/pre-recruitment achievements. Coached medals and personal medals never mix.

### Key Decisions & Rationale
- **Single Source of Truth for PNO:** Rather than relaxing PNO uniqueness everywhere, `UniquePnoAcrossPeople` explicitly ignores only the linked identity (`$ignoreMemberId`), and `StoreCoachRequest` enforces that `pno` matches the linked member. This guarantees cross-table uniqueness across all distinct personnel.
- **Hybrid Playing Achievements:** Coaches who joined police through sports quotas retain pre-recruitment national/state medals (`CoachPlayingAchievement`), but once recruited and competing in departmental tournaments, those medals flow from official `Achievement` records. The UI presents both under clearly separated sub-headers.

## Addendum — Team Event Medals Resolution for Members & Player-Coaches

### Scope & User Request
The user reported:
> *"okay lets move to the specific coach achivement and memeber who is a coach acheivement tabs I have added a team event under tourmaments and participants are a coach and and memeber so the member showing that medal in member achivement tab but when we opening coach profile as memeber not showing that and when check in the coach profile achevement tab under Departmental Tournament Medals not showing I have addded the event as team event for individual events medal is showing properly"*

### Root Cause Analysis
1. For team events, `Participation` stores `team_id` and an array of participant IDs in `lineup_member_ids` (while `member_id` is `null`).
2. Previously, multiple profile/payload controllers (`MemberProfileData::achievementsPayload`, `MemberProfileData::participationsPayload`, `CoachProfileData::memberPlayingAchievementsPayload`, `CoachPreviewController`, `MemberPromotionController`, `MemberAchievementsController`, etc.) queried achievements solely through `where('member_id', $member->id)` or `orWhereIn('team_id', TeamMember::where('member_id', $member->id)->pluck('team_id'))`.
3. Consequently:
   - A coach who participated in a team event had their ID recorded in `lineup_member_ids`, but was assigned as a coach (`CoachAssignment`), not a `TeamMember`.
   - The query ignored `lineup_member_ids` and ignored coach-assigned teams, causing the team event medal to disappear from both the coach's linked member profile and the coach profile's "Departmental Tournament Medals" (`playingAchievements`) section.

### Changes Implemented
1. **Centralized Query Scopes (`Participation::scopeForMember` & `Achievement::scopeForMember`):**
   - Added `scopeForMember(Builder $query, Member|int $member): Builder` to `app/Models/Participation.php`:
     - Matches `participations.member_id = $memberId` (individual events).
     - Matches `orWhereJsonContains('participations.lineup_member_ids', $memberId)` (team events with lineups).
     - Matches fallback squad participations without a specific lineup when the member belongs to that team as a player (`TeamMember`) or as a coach (`CoachAssignment`).
   - Added `scopeForMember(Builder $query, Member|int $member): Builder` to `app/Models/Achievement.php` as `whereHas('participation', fn ($q) => $q->forMember($member))`.

2. **Cleaned and Unified Profile Payloads:**
   - `app/Support/Members/MemberProfileData.php`:
     - Refactored `achievementsPayload` to use `Achievement::forMember($member)`.
     - Refactored `participationsPayload` to use `Participation::forMember($member)`.
     - Refactored `memberParticipationIds` to use `Participation::forMember($member)->pluck('id')`.
   - `app/Support/Coaches/CoachProfileData.php`:
     - Refactored `memberPlayingAchievementsPayload` to use `Achievement::forMember($member)`.
   - `app/Http/Controllers/Api/V1/CoachPreviewController.php`:
